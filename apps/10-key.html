What 10-Key does

10-Key replaces a physical adding machine on your phone. It's built for anyone who needs to quickly total a column of numbers by hand: bookkeepers, accountants, small business owners, or anyone adding up a receipt pile or a bank statement. Every number you enter and add or subtract is recorded, in order, on an on-screen paper tape, so you can scroll back through what you've entered, review it, label it, or correct a past entry, all while the app keeps a running total for you.

10-Key+ (the optional paid tier) adds multiply and divide, tax calculations, saved and named tapes you can switch between, and an optional Cloud Sync feature that backs your saved tapes up to your own Google Drive account.
