10-Key Calculator Privacy Policy

Effective July 24, 2026

10-Key Calculator does not collect, share, sell, or transmit any of your data. Everything the app stores stays on your own device.

What the app stores, and where

Your calculation history and your app settings (such as tax rate or decimal places) are saved only in local files on your device. Nothing you enter into the app is ever sent anywhere, including to the developer.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in of any kind
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No ads or ad networks
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, or other device data
  • No data shared with third parties

Permissions

The published app requests no device permissions. (An internet permission appears only in development builds used for testing, and is not part of the app you install from Google Play.)

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to me@will-i-am.dev.
