10-Key Calculator Privacy Policy

Effective August 7, 2026

10-Key Calculator does not collect, share, or sell your calculation data. Your tapes and settings stay on your own device, unless you turn on the optional Cloud Backup feature described below. The app does send basic crash reports and app-usage analytics, described in Crash Reports & Analytics below.

What the app stores, and where

Your calculation history and your app settings (such as tax rate or decimal places) are saved only in local files on your device. Nothing you enter into the app is sent anywhere, including to the developer, unless you turn on Cloud Backup.

Cloud Backup (optional, off by default)

10-Key+ has an optional Cloud Backup feature. It is off unless you turn it on yourself in Settings. When you turn it on:

  • You sign in with your own Google account.
  • Your saved tapes are backed up to a hidden, app-only folder in your own Google Drive. This folder is not visible in your regular Google Drive and cannot be opened by any other app.
  • Only you can see this data. The developer never has access to it.
  • No data other than your tape contents is sent to Google for this feature.
  • You can turn Cloud Backup off at any time in Settings.

Crash Reports & Analytics

10-Key Calculator uses Google's Firebase service to send crash reports and a small number of usage events back to the developer. This helps find and fix bugs, and shows whether things like the 10-Key+ purchase screen and the in-app tutorial are working as intended.

  • If the app crashes, a report is sent automatically with technical details such as the error, your device model, and the app version. This never includes your tape contents, labels, or amounts.
  • A small number of named events are logged: viewing the 10-Key+ upgrade screen, starting a purchase, completing a purchase, and finishing the tutorial. Basic device and app info that Firebase collects automatically, like app version and general region, may be attached to these too.
  • This data is used only to improve the app. It is not linked to your name or Google account, and it is never sold.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in unless you turn on Cloud Backup yourself
  • No tape contents, labels, or amounts are ever included in crash reports or analytics
  • No ads or ad networks
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, or other device data
  • No data shared with third parties, other than your own Google Drive (if you turn on Cloud Backup) and Google's Firebase service (crash reports and analytics, described above)

Permissions

The published app requests network access, used for the optional Cloud Backup feature above and for the crash reports and analytics described above. If you never turn on Cloud Backup, the app's only network connections are those crash reports and analytics events.

10-Key+ also has an optional floating calculator that you can start yourself from a button in the app. It uses Android's "display over other apps" permission so it can show a small calculator on top of whatever else you're looking at. This permission only controls what's shown on your own screen. It does not give the app any access to other apps' data, and turning it on does not send anything anywhere. You only see the permission prompt if you choose to try the floating calculator.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new effective date above.
